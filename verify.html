Historical declaration verification

Read-only, without wallet connection, signing or transactions. This separate page permits locally processing voluntarily disclosed real identity/bank identifiers. Existing creation pages remain synthetic-only. This tool checks only historical SDT v1 with four strings, not current 13-field SDT v2. An unsalted public hash can be guessed; enter sensitive details only if you understand that risk. The four original fields stay in this browser tab; only public wallet and contract addresses and optional transaction hash are sent to the selected RPC.

Exact v1 serialization

fullName|dateOfBirth|passportNumber|bankTransactionId

Join the four exact strings with ASCII |, encode as UTF-8 bytes (no newline), SHA-256. No trimming, case conversion, Unicode normalization or silent date correction. Date must be a real DD.MM.YYYY calendar date.

Check acceptance

This identifier may refer to any participant operation documentally connected with them; no special SDT payment is required. Its authenticity is not checked.

Technical result

No check performed.

A match is not identity verification, confirmation of a bank operation, proof of rights or a guarantee of admissibility in court. Documentary linkage may separately require passport documents, a bank record linking the operation to an identified customer, evidence of wallet use at the relevant moment, and a representative's authority where applicable. None are uploaded or automatically checked.
