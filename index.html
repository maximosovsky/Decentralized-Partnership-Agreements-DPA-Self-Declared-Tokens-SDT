Identity basis, agreement and participation

A reusable Self-Declared Token (SDT) links a wallet to a declaration; it is not a verified passport or unique-person certificate. A Decentralized Partnership Agreement (DPA) records a version and hash of separate off-chain terms, named participant wallets and shares, and their individual acceptances. The separate pre-authenticated route described in the research is not implemented.

Preserve a basis for later attribution

A signature alone does not identify a person. A participant has a reason to retain evidence connecting a wallet, declaration and assent if rights are disputed. A self-declared banking transaction identifier may support later documentary tracing; no bank issues or verifies the SDT. Neither attribution nor enforceable rights are guaranteed.

SDT declaration hashing

New SDT v2 hashes bind all 13 exact entered synthetic fields: name, birth date, passport number, transaction number, bank name, SWIFT/BIC, account number, transaction date, time, time zone, amount, currency and recipient. Historical SDT v1 records use only the original four fields and are unchanged. These fields are not independently verified and do not establish that a bank or transfer exists.
